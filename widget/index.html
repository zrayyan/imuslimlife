Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. The widget is free, has no ads and needs no account.

Does it update by itself?

Yes. It shows today's times every day, with the next prayer and a countdown, in the city's time zone.

Can it show our iqamah times?

Not yet. Masjids that join Masjid Connect can publish their iqamah and Jumu'ah times for the Muslim Companion Alexa skill; email info@yzinfotech.com.
