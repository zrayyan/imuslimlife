Privacy Policy for iMuslimLife

Last Updated: March 19, 2026

iMuslimLife ("we", "us", "our") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have when using iMuslimLife websites, mobile experiences, API services, and connected assistant integrations (including Amazon Alexa and Google Home/Nest related flows).

Canonical policy URL for platform verification: https://imuslimlife.com/privacy-policy/

Information We Collect

Personal Information

We may collect personal information when you create an account, contact support, request services, or link supported assistant accounts, such as:

  • Name and email address (for account and support communication)
  • Organization/company name (if provided)
  • Profile or account identifiers needed to provide account-linked features
  • Support messages and related contact details

Technical Information

We may automatically collect technical information including:

  • IP address and general device information
  • Browser type and operating system
  • Pages visited and actions taken on our site
  • Service and API usage metrics

Location Data

We process location information (for example latitude/longitude, timezone, or city-level information) when needed to calculate prayer times and location-based Islamic features. We do not sell precise location data.

Connected Assistants and Smart Home Data

If you use assistant integrations (such as Amazon Alexa or Google Home/Nest), we process the minimum data required to fulfill your requests and automation behavior (for example linked account identifiers, requested prayer event, and command metadata). We do not use this data to build advertising profiles.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain requested services
  • To process and manage your account and API usage
  • To improve user experience, reliability, and security
  • To send service notices, policy updates, and security communications
  • To monitor usage patterns and prevent abuse
  • To deliver assistant-related responses and smart home automations you request

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information.

We may share information in the following limited scenarios:

  • Service Providers: Vendors who help us operate services under appropriate safeguards
  • Integration Partners: Assistant platform data exchange needed for authentication and fulfillment
  • Legal Requirements: Where required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide requested services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Retention periods vary by data type and purpose.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No internet transmission or storage method is 100% secure.

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to:

  • Access and review personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of personal information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities
  • Request account unlinking/deletion for connected assistant integrations

To exercise these rights, contact: privacy@imuslimlife.com

Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to support essential functionality, understand usage, and improve experience. You can manage cookies through browser settings, though some features may be limited if disabled.

International Transfers

Depending on where you access our services, your information may be processed in countries other than your own. We apply reasonable safeguards to protect personal information.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. When we do, we revise the "Last Updated" date above.
