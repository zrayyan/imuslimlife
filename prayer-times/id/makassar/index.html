Questions

How are the prayer times for Makassar calculated?

With the Kementerian Agama, Indonesia method and the standard (Shafi'i, Maliki, Hanbali) time for Asr, for Makassar's coordinates and local time zone. Your masjid's timetable may differ by a few minutes.

Which direction is the Qibla in Makassar?

The Qibla in Makassar is 292° from true north, roughly west-northwest. Makkah is about 9,148 km away.

When does Suhoor end and when is Iftar in Makassar?

Suhoor ends at Fajr and Iftar is at Maghrib. Both are in the monthly timetables for every day of the year.
