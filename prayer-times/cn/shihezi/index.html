Questions

How are the prayer times for Shihezi calculated?

With the Muslim World League method and the standard (Shafi'i, Maliki, Hanbali) time for Asr, for Shihezi's coordinates and local time zone. Your masjid's timetable may differ by a few minutes.

Which direction is the Qibla in Shihezi?

The Qibla in Shihezi is 254° from true north, roughly west-southwest. Makkah is about 4,922 km away.

When does Suhoor end and when is Iftar in Shihezi?
