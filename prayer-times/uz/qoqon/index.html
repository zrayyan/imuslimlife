How are the prayer times for Qo'qon calculated?

With the Muslim World League method and the Hanafi time for Asr, for Qo'qon's coordinates and local time zone. Your masjid's timetable may differ by a few minutes.

The Qibla in Qo'qon is 243° from true north, roughly west-southwest. Makkah is about 3,617 km away.
