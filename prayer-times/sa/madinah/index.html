Questions

How are the prayer times for Madinah calculated?

With the Umm al-Qura University, Makkah method and the standard (Shafi'i, Maliki, Hanbali) time for Asr, for Madinah's coordinates and local time zone. Your masjid's timetable may differ by a few minutes.

Which direction is the Qibla in Madinah?

The Qibla in Madinah is 176° from true north, roughly south. Makkah is about 339 km away.

When does Suhoor end and when is Iftar in Madinah?

Suhoor ends at Fajr and Iftar is at Maghrib. Both are in the monthly timetables for every day of the year.
