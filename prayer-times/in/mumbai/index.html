Questions

How are the prayer times for Mumbai calculated?

With the University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi method and the Hanafi time for Asr, for Mumbai's coordinates and local time zone. Your masjid's timetable may differ by a few minutes.

Which direction is the Qibla in Mumbai?

The Qibla in Mumbai is 280° from true north, roughly west. Makkah is about 3,452 km away.

When does Suhoor end and when is Iftar in Mumbai?

Suhoor ends at Fajr and Iftar is at Maghrib. Both are in the monthly timetables for every day of the year.
