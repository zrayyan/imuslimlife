Ramadan 2027 (1448 AH)

Expected dates, Suhoor and Iftar times, and Ramadan timetables for 500 cities

Ramadan beginsMon, February 8
Eid al-FitrTue, March 9
First TaraweehSun, February 7

 

Expected dates

By the calculated Umm al-Qura calendar, the first day of Ramadan 1448 is Monday, February 8, 2027 and Eid al-Fitr is Tuesday, March 9, 2027. The month begins with the sighting of the new moon, so where your community follows a local sighting, Ramadan and Eid may begin a day later. Each city's timetable below shows its own calculated Hijri dates.

Taraweeh is prayed on the night before each fast: the first Taraweeh is on the evening of February 7, 2027.

Suhoor and Iftar

Suhoor, the meal before dawn, ends at Fajr. Iftar, breaking the fast, is at Maghrib. Both times change a little every day; your city's Ramadan timetable lists them for the whole month, and the timetables print on one page.

The last ten nights

Laylat al-Qadr is sought in the odd nights of the last ten. Each night begins at Maghrib on the evening before the day:

  • Night 21evening of Saturday, February 27
  • Night 23evening of Monday, March 1
  • Night 25evening of Wednesday, March 3
  • Night 27evening of Friday, March 5
  • Night 29evening of Sunday, March 7

Questions

When does Ramadan 2027 start?

By the calculated Umm al-Qura calendar, Ramadan 1448 begins on Monday, February 8, 2027, so the first Taraweeh is on the evening of February 7, 2027 and the first fast that day. The actual start depends on the moon sighting your community follows and can be a day later.

When is Eid al-Fitr 2027?

By the same calendar, Eid al-Fitr is on Tuesday, March 9, 2027, depending on the sighting of the Shawwal moon.
