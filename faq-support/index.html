Frequently Asked Questions & Support

How are the prayer times calculated?

We use highly accurate calculation methods based on astronomical formulas. You can choose from various calculation methods in the settings to match your local mosque or preference.

Is my location data stored?

We prioritize your privacy. Location data is only used temporarily to fetch prayer times and is not stored on our servers unless you explicitly save a location.

Can I use this app offline?

Basic features like the Quran reader might work offline if previously loaded. However, fetching prayer times for new locations or dates requires an internet connection.

How can I report an issue or suggest a feature?

We'd love to hear from you! Please use the contact button below or reach out to us via our support channels mentioned in the contact section.

Is the app completely free?

Yes, iMuslim Life is 100% free and ad-free. Our goal is to provide essential Islamic tools accessible to everyone.
