About iMuslim Life

iMuslim Life is the website of Salat-e-Mustaqeem, a prayer app for iPhone, Apple Watch and Android, and of Muslim Companion, which plays the Athan on Amazon Echo. Both are made by a small independent team at YZ Infotech.

Everything here is free and always ad-free: no ads, no tracking for advertising, no account needed to see prayer times.

What is on this site

  • Prayer times for 500 cities in 82 countries, with monthly timetables and Hijri dates.
  • Qibla direction and the Islamic calendar.
  • A masjid directory for the USA and Canada, and a free prayer-times widget for masjid websites.
  • The Quran with recitation and translation.

How the times are calculated

Each city uses the calculation method of its country's religious authority. The sources, methods and Hijri dates are explained in full on the methodology page.
