How it works

  1. Enable the skill in the Alexa app and link your Amazon account.
  2. Choose your settings at alexa.imuslimlife.com: your location, calculation method, Asr time, Fajr Athan, and what plays after the Athan.
  3. Create one Alexa routine that starts when the Muslim Companion Azan sensor opens. The skill opens that sensor at each prayer time.
  4. Hear the Athan on the Echo you picked, five times a day, adjusted for your location every day.

Nothing is set up by voice. The dashboard works on your phone or computer, and you can change a setting any time; your next Athan uses it.

Everything Muslim Companion does on Alexa

🌙 Ramadan modeA Suhoor reminder, Iftar at Maghrib, and Makkah Taraweeh recitation after Isha.
🧠 Hifz CoachMemorize a few ayahs at a time, with repetition and saved progress.
📺 Echo ShowToday's prayer times on screen with a countdown to the next prayer, plus a widget.

Accurate times for where you live

Prayer times are calculated for your exact location each day. On the dashboard you choose the calculation method your community follows, such as ISNA, Muslim World League, Umm al-Qura or Karachi, and the Asr time (Hanafi or standard). If you travel, update your location and the next Athan follows you.

Questions

Can Alexa play the Athan automatically?

Yes. Alexa has no built-in Athan, but with the free Muslim Companion skill your Echo plays the Athan at your local prayer times for all five prayers. You set it up once with a single Alexa routine.

Is Muslim Companion free?

Yes. The skill and the dashboard are free, with no ads and no subscription.

Which Echo devices does it work on?

Every Echo speaker and Echo Show. On an Echo Show you also see today's prayer times on screen.

Which countries is it available in?

The skill is available in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, India and Australia in English, and in Arabic and Hindi.

Do I have to set it up by voice?

No. You choose your location and settings at alexa.imuslimlife.com from your phone or computer. Voice is only for asking about prayer times, Quran and Hifz.
