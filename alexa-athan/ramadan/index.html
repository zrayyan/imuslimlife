Ramadan mode

Say “Alexa, ask Muslim Companion to turn on Ramadan mode”, or switch it on at alexa.imuslimlife.com. It adds three things to your automatic Athan:

  • Suhoor reminder before Fajr, so you know when to stop eating.
  • Iftar announced with the Maghrib Athan.
  • Taraweeh: recitation from Makkah after Isha.

Ask “Alexa, ask Muslim Companion is Ramadan mode on” to check, and turn it off after Eid the same way.

Suhoor and Iftar times

Say “Alexa, ask Muslim Companion when is suhoor”. Alexa tells you when Suhoor ends (Fajr) and when Iftar is (Maghrib), for your location.

When is Ramadan?

Ask “Alexa, ask Muslim Companion when is Ramadan” for a countdown. It also knows Eid, Arafah, Ashura, the last ten nights and the white days. Dates follow Umm al-Qura and can differ by a day where the moon is sighted locally.

Questions

Does Ramadan mode change my Athan times?

No. Your prayer times stay the same; Ramadan mode adds the Suhoor reminder, the Iftar announcement and Taraweeh.

Do I need a new routine for Ramadan?

No. Ramadan mode runs through your existing Athan routine.
