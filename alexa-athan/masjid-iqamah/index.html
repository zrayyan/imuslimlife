Follow your masjid

On the dashboard, open My Masjid and follow your masjid. Muslim Companion then uses the iqamah and Jumu'ah times your masjid publishes, and can remind you on your Echo before the iqamah.

Ask any time:

“Alexa, ask Muslim Companion when is iqamah”

“Alexa, ask Muslim Companion when is jumuah”

Asking for your next prayer also includes your masjid's iqamah.

Is your masjid not listed yet?

Masjid Connect is new, and masjids join it themselves. Ask your masjid to email info@yzinfotech.com. It's free for masjids, and their congregation hears the right iqamah and Jumu'ah times at home.

Questions

What's the difference between prayer time and iqamah?

The prayer time is when the prayer starts (the Athan). The iqamah is when the congregational prayer begins at the masjid, set by the masjid.

Does it cost the masjid anything?

No. Masjid Connect is free.
