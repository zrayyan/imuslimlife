Prayer times on your Echo Show

On an Echo Show, Muslim Companion shows today's prayer times on screen with the next prayer and a countdown, and adds a Prayer Times widget to your home screen.

Today's prayer times on screen

Say “Alexa, ask Muslim Companion for today's prayer times”. Alexa reads them, and your Echo Show displays all five with the next prayer highlighted and a countdown to it.

The Prayer Times widget

Add the Muslim Companion Prayer Times widget from your Echo Show's widget gallery to keep the next prayer on your home screen. It updates through the day from your saved location.

More on the screen

  • Hifz Coach: each ayah appears on screen while you memorize. Say “Alexa, ask Muslim Companion to start Hifz”.
  • Tasbih: tap the screen to count your dhikr.

The automatic Athan works the same on an Echo Show as on any Echo: set it up once with a routine.

Questions

Does the Athan play on an Echo Show?

Yes. Pick the Echo Show under Hear Alexa from in your Athan routine.

Which location does the widget use?

Your saved location from the dashboard, or your Echo's address if you said "use my Echo's location".

Get the Athan on your Echo today

Enable the skill, then choose your settings on the dashboard. It takes about five minutes.
