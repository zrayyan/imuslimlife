Dua for entering and leaving the toilet

Daily duas · 2 duas in Arabic with transliteration, meaning and source

Dua for entering and leaving the toilet (1)

(بِسْمِ اللَّهِ). اللَّهُـمَّ إِنِّي أَعُـوذُ بِـكَ مِـنَ الْخُـبْثِ وَالْخَبَائِثِ.

(Bismil-lah) allahumma innee aAAoothu bika minal-khubthi wal-khaba-ith

(In the name of Allah). O Allah, I take refuge with you from all evil and evil-doers.

Source: Al-Bukhari 1:45, Muslim 1:283

Dua for entering and leaving the toilet (2)

غُفْرَانَكَ.

Ghufranak

I ask You (Allah) for forgiveness.

Source: Zadul-Ma'ad 2:387
