Dua for Allah’s help (3)

حَسْبُـنا اللهُ وَنِعْـمَ الوَكـيل

Hasbunallaahu wa ni'amal-wakeel

Allah is sufficient for us and the best of those on whom to depend.

Source: Surah Al-Imran 3:173
