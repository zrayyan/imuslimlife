Dua for righteous company

Everyday life · 4 duas in Arabic with transliteration, meaning and source

Dua for righteous company (1)

فَاطِرَ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضِ أَنْتَ وَلِيِّي فِي الدُّنْيَا وَالْآخِرَةِ ۖ تَوَفَّنِي مُسْلِمًا وَأَلْحِقْنِي بِالصَّالِحِينَ

fatira assamawatiwal-ardi anta waliyyee fee addunyawal-akhirati tawaffanee musliman waalhiqneebissaliheen

Creator of the heavens and earth, You are my protector in this world and in the Hereafter. Cause me to die a Muslim and join me with the righteous.

Source: Surah Yusuf - 12:101

Dua for righteous company (2)

رَبَّنَا آمَنَّا فَاكْتُبْنَا مَعَ الشَّاهِدِينَ

Rabbana aamana faktubna ma' ash-shahideen

Our Lord! We believe; write us down among the witnesses.

Source: Surah Al-Ma'ida - 5:83
