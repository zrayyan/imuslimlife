Ramadan duas (special occasions)

Special occasions · 11 duas in Arabic with transliteration, meaning and source

Ramadan duas (special occasions) (1)

اَللَّهُمَّ اِنَّكَ عَفُوٌّ ، تُحِبُّ الْعَفْوَ فَاعْفُ عَنِّي

Allahumma innaka `afuwwun tuhibbul `afwa fa`fu `annee

O Allah You are The One Who forgives greatly, and loves to forgive, so forgive me

Source: Ibn Majah: 3850, At-Tirmidhi: 3513

Ramadan duas (special occasions) (2)

اللَّهُمَّ أَهْلِلْهُ عَلَيْنَا بِالْيُمْنِ وَالإِيمَانِ وَالسَّلاَمَةِ وَالإِسْلاَمِ رَبِّي وَرَبُّكَ اللَّهُ

Allahumma ahlilhu `alainā bil-yumni wal-iman, was-salamati wal-Islam, rabbi wa rabbuk Allah

O Allah, bring it over us with blessing and faith, and security and Islam. My Lord and your Lord is Allah.

Source: Tirmidhi: 3451

Ramadan duas (special occasions) (3)

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ

Bismillah

In the name of Allah

Ramadan duas (special occasions) (4)

وَبِصَوْمِ غَدٍ نَّوَيْتُ مِنْ شَهْرِ رَمَضَانَ

Wa bisawmi ghadinn nawaiytu min shahri Ramadan

I intend to keep the fast for tomorrow in the month of Ramadan

Ramadan duas (special occasions) (5)

ذَهَبَ الظَّمَأُ وَابْتَلَّتِ الْعُرُوقُ وَثَبَتَ الأَجْرُ إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ

Dhahaba al-zama’ wa abtalat al-‘urooq wa thabata al-ajr in sha Allaah

Thirst is gone, the veins are moistened and the reward is certain if Allaah wills.

Source: Abu Dawud: 2357

Ramadan duas (special occasions) (6)

اللَّهُمَّ لَكَ صُمْتُ وَعَلَى رِزْقِكَ أَفْطَرْتُ

Allahumma inni laka sumtu wa ala rizq-ika-aftartu

O Allah! For You I have fasted and upon your provision, I have broken my fast.

Source: Abu Dawud: 2358

Ramadan duas (special occasions) (7)

أَفْطَرَ عِنْدَكُمُ الصَّائِمُونَ وَأَكَلَ طَعَامَكُمُ الأَبْرَارُ وَصَلَّتْ عَلَيْكُمُ الْمَلاَئِكَةُ

Aftara ‘indakumus-saimun, wa akala ta’amakumul-abrar, wa sallat ‘alaikumul- mala’ikah

May the fasting (men) break their fast with you, and the pious eat your food, and the angels pray for blessing on you

Source: Ibn Majah: 1747

Ramadan duas (special occasions) (8)

يَا حَيُّ يَا قَيُّومُ بِرَحْمَتِكَ أَسْتَغيثُ

Ya Hayyu Ya Qayyum bi rehmatika astaghees

Oh Everliving, The Everlasting, I seek Your help through Your mercy.

Ramadan duas (special occasions) (9)

اَسْتَغْفِرُ اللہَ رَبِّی مِنْ کُلِّ زَنْبٍ وَّ اَتُوْبُ اِلَیْہِ

Astagfirullaha rab-bi min kulli zambiyon wa-atoobuilaiyh

I seek forgiveness from Allah for all my sins and turn to Him.

Ramadan duas (special occasions) (10)

اَللَّهُمَّ أَجِرْنِي مِنَ النَّارِ

Allahumma Ajirni minan naar

O Allah, save me from the fire (Jahannam).

Ramadan duas (special occasions) (11)

اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّكَ عَفُوٌّ كَرِيمٌ تُحِبُّ الْعَفْوَ فَاعْفُ عَنِّي

Allahumma innaka Afuwwun [Karimun ] tuhibbul `afwa fa`fu `annee

O Allah, indeed You are Pardoning, [Generous,] You love pardon, so pardon me

Source: Tirmidhi: 3513
