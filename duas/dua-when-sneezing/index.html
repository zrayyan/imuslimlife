Dua when sneezing

Daily duas · 3 duas in Arabic with transliteration, meaning and source

Dua when sneezing (1)

اَلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ

Alhamdu lillaahi

All praise is for Allah.

Source: Al-Bukhari 7:125

Dua when sneezing (2)

يَرْحَمُكَ اللَّهُ

Yar hamu kal-laahu.

May Allah have mercy upon you

Source: Al-Bukhari 7:126

Dua when sneezing (3)

يَهْدِيكُمُ اللَّهُ وَيُصْلِحُ بَالَكُمْ

Yahdeekumul-lahu wayuslihu balakum

May Allah guide you and rectify your condition.

Source: Sahih Al-Bukhari: 6224
