Questions

What is the Islamic date today?

The date at the top of this page is today’s Hijri date in your own time zone, from the Umm al-Qura calendar. With a local moon sighting it may be a day later.

How is the Hijri calendar worked out?

The Hijri year has 12 lunar months of 29 or 30 days, about 354 days in all. Each month begins with the new crescent; these pages use the calculated Umm al-Qura calendar of Saudi Arabia.

Why does my masjid’s date differ?

Many communities begin a month when the crescent is seen locally, so their date can be one day behind a calculated calendar. Follow your masjid for Ramadan and Eid.
