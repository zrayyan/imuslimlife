Isra and Mi’raj 2026

Friday, January 16, 2026 · 27 Rajab 1447 AH

About Isra and Mi’raj

Many Muslims remember the Prophet’s ﷺ night journey to Jerusalem and ascension on the night of 27 Rajab; the exact date is not agreed upon. The night begins at Maghrib on Thursday, January 15.

Dates follow the calculated Umm al-Qura calendar. Where your community follows a local moon sighting, the month, and so the event, may begin a day later; follow your masjid.
