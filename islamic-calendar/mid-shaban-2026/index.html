15 Sha’ban 2026

Tuesday, February 3, 2026 · 15 Shaban 1447 AH

About 15 Sha’ban

The night of the 15th of Sha’ban, which many Muslims spend in prayer and supplication. Practice differs between communities and schools. The night begins at Maghrib on Monday, February 2.

Dates follow the calculated Umm al-Qura calendar. Where your community follows a local moon sighting, the month, and so the event, may begin a day later; follow your masjid.

Questions

When is 15 Sha’ban 2026?

By the calculated Umm al-Qura calendar, 15 Sha’ban 2026 is on Tuesday, February 3, 2026 (15 Shaban 1447 AH). The night begins at Maghrib on Monday, February 2. Where the month begins with a local moon sighting it may be a day later.
