Start of Ramadan 2026

Wednesday, February 18, 2026 · 1 Ramadan 1447 AH

About Start of Ramadan

The first day of fasting. Taraweeh begins the evening before; Suhoor ends at Fajr and Iftar is at Maghrib.

Dates follow the calculated Umm al-Qura calendar. Where your community follows a local moon sighting, the month, and so the event, may begin a day later; follow your masjid.
