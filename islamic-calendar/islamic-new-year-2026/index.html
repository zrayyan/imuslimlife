Islamic New Year 2026

Tuesday, June 16, 2026 · 1 Muharram 1448 AH

About Islamic New Year

The first day of Muharram begins the new Hijri year, counted from the Prophet’s ﷺ migration (Hijrah) from Makkah to Madinah.

Dates follow the calculated Umm al-Qura calendar. Where your community follows a local moon sighting, the month, and so the event, may begin a day later; follow your masjid.
