Eid al-Adha 2027

Sunday, May 16, 2027 · 10 Dhu al-Hijjah 1448 AH

About Eid al-Adha

The festival of sacrifice, during Hajj. The Eid prayer is after sunrise and the Udhiyah (Qurbani) follows it, through the 13th of Dhul Hijjah.

Dates follow the calculated Umm al-Qura calendar. Where your community follows a local moon sighting, the month, and so the event, may begin a day later; follow your masjid.
