Questions

Good to <e>know</e>.

What does “Niyyah” mean?

Niyyah (نِيَّة) is intention. “Actions are only by intentions” (Sahih al-Bukhari 1). Niyyah helps an intention become a small, steady action.

Do I need an account?

No account is needed to try Quran Echoes. To keep a plan, mark the days you showed up and save passages, sign in with Amazon, the same sign-in as Muslim Companion for Alexa.

Which languages does it understand?

English, Urdu, Roman Urdu (like “mujhe bohat ghussa aata hai”) and Arabic. Passages show the Arabic with an English or Urdu translation.

Is it a replacement for a scholar or counsellor?

No. Niyyah offers passages to reflect on and small steps to take. For rulings, ask a qualified scholar; in hard times, please reach out to someone you trust or a helpline.

Where does it work?

In any browser on your phone, tablet or computer, at alexa.imuslimlife.com/niyyah.
