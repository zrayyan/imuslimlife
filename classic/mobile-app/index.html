Frequently asked questions

How accurate are the prayer times?

Times are calculated for your exact location. Choose the calculation method and the Asr school (Shafiʿi or Hanafi) that your masjid follows. When you travel, the times and the reminders follow you.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Prayer times are calculated on the phone when there is no connection, and the Quran text, word meanings and the ayahs you have listened to stay available offline.

Is Salat-e-Mustaqeem free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. We believe prayer tools should be open to everyone.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later, Apple Watch with watchOS 11 or later (it comes with the iPhone app), Android phones with Android 7.0 or later, and Galaxy Watch and other Wear OS watches (they come with the Android app).

How do I add a widget?

On iPhone, touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Salat-e-Mustaqeem. On Android, touch and hold the home screen, tap Widgets and choose Salat-e-Mustaqeem. Pick Next Prayer, Today’s Prayers or Current Prayer.

Which languages does the app speak?

The app is available in English, Arabic and Urdu (Settings → Language), with right-to-left layout. Word meanings are in English and Urdu, and Siri understands English and Arabic.

Is there an Alexa skill?

Yes, Muslim Companion. It plays the Athan on your Echo at every prayer, recites any surah, coaches your Hifz and keeps Ramadan. Say “Alexa, open Muslim Companion”.
