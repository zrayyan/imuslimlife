How it works

Three moments, and nothing between you and the Quran

Tasmīʿ is the word a hifz teacher uses for “recite it back to me”. It listens the way a patient teacher does: it follows, it notices, and it waits.

Recite

Open any ayah in the reader, or a Hifz lesson, and tap the lamp. Recite at your own pace, the way you would to your teacher.

The page follows

The word you are saying glows. The page turns by itself. When the ayah ends, you see what to review: a soft wave under a skipped word, amber under one said differently.

Hear the word

Tap a marked word and hear just that word from the qari’s own recitation. Then recite the ayah again. No scores, ever: only “2 words to review”.

For hifz

Test me: the page stays veiled until you say it

From any Hifz lesson, turn on Test me. The ayah is hidden, and each word is revealed only as you recite it correctly. It is the closest thing to sitting before your teacher, at any hour of the night.

Experimental

The Muʿallim: tajweed, held to your own pace

A small listening model we are building ourselves, on the phone like the rest of Tasmīʿ. It times each madd and ghunnah in ḥarakāt measured from your own recitation, so a slow murattal and a quick ḥadr are judged alike, and it marks the places a teacher would pencil in.

It begins with madd and ghunnah, then qalqalah. When it is unsure, it says nothing: a correct recitation should never be marked wrong. It will not replace your teacher; it helps you arrive prepared.

Madd munfaṣil: held 2, asks 4–5

It checks words, not tajweed

Tasmīʿ tells you whether you said the right words in the right order. It does not judge makharij, madd or other rules of tajweed, and it is not a teacher. Please recite to a teacher too. Tasmīʿ is marked Experimental while we keep improving it.

Questions

Before you begin

Is my recitation recorded or uploaded?

No. Listening happens entirely on your phone. Audio is never stored and never sent, to us or anyone.

Does it work without the internet?

Yes, once the 88 MB listening pack is on your phone. It downloads on Wi‑Fi by default; you can choose mobile data.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhone and Android phones that run Salat-e-Mustaqeem. On older phones it adjusts itself so the gold light keeps up.

Can it check my tajweed?

Not yet, but the Muʿallim is on its way. It will start with the lengths of madd and ghunnah, measured against your own pace, and stay silent whenever it is unsure. Until then, please recite to a teacher for tajweed.

Is it good for children?

Yes. There are no scores, only gentle marks. A Qaida mode for learning the letters is on its way.
