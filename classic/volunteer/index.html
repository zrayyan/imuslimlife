Volunteer with iMuslim Life

Volunteer with us

Your skills can become a sadaqah jariyah

Salat-e-Mustaqeem and iMuslim Life are free, with no ads, used every day by Muslims around the world. A few hours of your time can help someone pray on time, understand the Quran in their own language, or find their nearest masjid, again and again.

Email us to volunteer

“Whoever guides someone to goodness will have a reward like the one who does it.”

Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him), Sahih Muslim 1893

Every word you translate is read by people you will never meet. Every recitation you record is heard in homes, cars and masjids. We are a small team, and volunteers are how this work reaches every language and every corner of the world.

Ways you can help

Pick what fits you. No experience with apps needed, only sincerity, care and a little time.

I can help

Translators

Bring the app, the website, duas and weekly stories into your language: Arabic, Urdu, French, Spanish, Turkish, Indonesian, Malay, Bengali, Hindi and more. We check every translation before it goes live.

From 1–2 hours a week

Reciters

If Allah has blessed you with a beautiful voice and correct tajweed, record Quran recitations, duas or the Athan for listeners worldwide. We help with recording tips and editing.

Record at your own pace

Scholars and reviewers

Students of knowledge, imams and teachers: check translations, stories and quiz answers for accuracy and sources, so everything we publish is trustworthy.

Review when you can

Writers and storytellers

Help write the weekly stories of the Prophets, the Companions and the Seerah for families and children, in English, Urdu or another language, always from authentic sources.

One story a month is a big help

Testers

Try new features before release on iPhone, Android or Alexa, and tell us what feels wrong or confusing. Fresh eyes catch what we miss.

A few minutes per release

Masjid ambassadors

Help your local masjid publish its iqamah and Jumu’ah times so its community sees them in the app, on the website and on Alexa. Free for every masjid.

One conversation can serve a whole community

How it works

Email us

Tell us which role, which languages you know, and a little about yourself.

We reply personally

We get back to you, share a short guide and a first small piece of work.

Your work goes live

After review, your contribution reaches people around the world.

What you can expect from us

Your time, your pace

No minimum commitment. Help when you can, pause whenever you need to.

Credit if you want it

We are happy to thank you by name, or keep your help between you and Allah. Your choice.

Care for accuracy

Religious content is reviewed and sourced before it is published. You never carry that alone.

Your privacy respected

We use your email only to work with you, and never share it or add you to mailing lists.

Ready to help?

Send us one email. Even if you are not sure where you fit, write to us and we will find the right place together, in shaa Allah.

More from iMuslim Life

  • Accurate Prayer Times: Get accurate Islamic prayer times (Salat) for any location worldwide, with multiple calculation methods, Hanafi or standard Asr, and high-latitude adjustments.
  • Hijri Calendar and Islamic Dates: The Hijri (Islamic) calendar alongside the Gregorian calendar, with important Islamic dates through the year.
  • Read & Listen to the Quran: Read and listen to the Holy Quran online with translations, tafsir, Tajweed highlighting, word-by-word analysis, and audio recitations.
  • Ramadan 2027 Countdown: How Long Until Ramadan 1448?: A live countdown to Ramadan 1448 (expected 8 February 2027) under tonight's real moon. Walk the thirty nights, see what arrives in the Salat-e-Mustaqeem app, and get a reminder when the moon is sighted.
  • Salat-e-Mustaqeem Prayer Times App for iPhone and Android: Salat-e-Mustaqeem 1.07 for iPhone, Android, Apple Watch and Galaxy Watch: the full Athan, Quran offline, Journey for families, widgets and Siri. Free and ad-free.
  • Muslim Companion Alexa Skill: Athan on Your Echo: Muslim Companion plays the Athan on your Amazon Echo at your prayer times, with Quran, adhkar, Hifz Coach and Ramadan mode. Free. Set up at alexa.imuslimlife.com.
  • Free Prayer Times API: A free REST API for Islamic prayer times by coordinates, with calculation methods and Asr school options. Documentation and examples.
  • Qibla Direction Finder - Find the Qibla from Anywhere: Find the exact Qibla direction to the Kaaba in Makkah from your location or any city, with a live phone compass, degrees from true north and distance. Free and ad-free.
  • Support & FAQ: Find answers to frequently asked questions about iMuslim Life prayer times, features, and get support.
  • Volunteer: Help Muslims everywhere pray on time and read the Quran in their own language. Volunteer as a translator, reciter, reviewer, writer, tester or masjid ambassador.
  • AI Agents: Ask Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Copilot for today's prayer times, Qibla and Hijri date. Connect our free MCP server in a minute, no API key needed.
  • Niyyah: Turn an Intention into a Small, Steady Step: Niyyah turns an intention into a 7-day plan anchored to your prayer times, and Quran Echoes finds scholar-reviewed Quran passages for what's on your heart. Free, no ads.
  • Tasmīʿ: Recite and the Page Follows You: Tasmīʿ in Salat-e-Mustaqeem listens as you recite the Quran: each word lights in gold, and skipped words are softly marked for review. On your phone, never recorded, works offline. Free.
  • Mihrab: Free Masjid TV Display for Prayer and Iqamah Times: Mihrab puts your masjid's prayer and Iqamah times, Jumu'ah live camera, notices, fundraising and Ramadan on every TV, 24/7. For Android TV and Fire TV. Free for every masjid.
