Dua before sleeping (7)

بِاسْـمِكَ اللّهُـمَّ أَمـوتُ وَأَحْـيا

Bismika Allāhumma amūtu wa aḥyā.

In Your Name, O Allah, I die and I live.

Source: Here, dying and living are metaphors for sleep and wakefulness. This explains why the normal order of these words has been reversed in this Hadith. In other contexts the living is mentioned before dying. See Qur'an Al-Baqarah 2:258, Aal-'Imrdn 3:156, Al-A'raf 7:158 among many other examples, (trans.). See also Al-Asqalani, Fathul-Bari 11/113, Muslim 4/ 2083.; Ḥiṣn al-Muslim 105
