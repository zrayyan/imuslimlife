Dua before sleeping (6)

اللّهُـمَّ قِنـي عَذابَـكَ يَـوْمَ تَبْـعَثُ عِبـادَك .

Allāhumma qinī `adhābaka yawma tab`athu `ibādak.

O Allah,1 save me from Your punishment on the Day that You resurrect Your slaves.2

Source: 1 "When the Prophet (ﷺ) wanted to lie down to sleep, he used to place his right hand under his cheek and say..." 2 Abu Dawud 4/311. See also Al-Albani, Sahih At-Tirmidhi 3/143.; Ḥiṣn al-Muslim 104
