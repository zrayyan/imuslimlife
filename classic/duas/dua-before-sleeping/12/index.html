Dua before sleeping (12)

يقرأ ((ألم * تنزيل)) السجدة، و((تبارك الذي بيده الملك))

-- Recite Surah 32 (As-Sajdah) and Surah 67 (Al-Mulk) in Arabic.

Source: At-Tirmidhi, An-Nasa'i. See also Al-Albani, Sahihul-Jami ' As-Saghir 4/255; Ḥiṣn al-Muslim 110
