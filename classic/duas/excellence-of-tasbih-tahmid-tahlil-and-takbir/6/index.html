Excellence of Tasbih, Tahmid, Tahlil, and Takbir (6)

مَنْ قَالَ (سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ الْعَظِيمِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ) غُرِسَتْ لَهُ نَخْلَةٌ فِي الْجَنَّةِ

Whoever says: Subḥānallāhi ‘l-'Aẓīmi wa biḥamdih will have a date palm planted for him in Paradise.

Whoever says: Glorified is Allah the Most Great and praised is He. will have a date palm planted for him in Paradise.

Source: At-Tirmidhi 5/511, and Al-Hakim who graded it authentic and Ath-Thahabi agreed 1/501. See also Al-Albani, Sahihul-Jami' As-Saghir 5/531 and Sahih At-Tirmidhi3/160.; Ḥiṣn al-Muslim 259
