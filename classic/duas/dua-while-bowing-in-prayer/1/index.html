Dua while bowing in prayer (1)

سُبْـحانَ رَبِّـيَ الْعَظـيم (ثلاث مرات)

Subḥāna Rabbiyal-`Aẓīm.[three times]

Glory to my Lord the Exalted (three times in Arabic)

Source: Abu Dawud, Ibn Majah, An-Nasa'i, At-Tirmidhi, and Ahmad. See Al-Albani's Sahih At-Tirmidhi 1/83.; Ḥiṣn al-Muslim 33
