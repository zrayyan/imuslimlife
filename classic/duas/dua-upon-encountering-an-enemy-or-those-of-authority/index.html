Dua upon encountering an enemy or those of authority

Fortress of the Muslim (Ḥiṣn al-Muslim) · 3 duas in Arabic with transliteration, meaning and source

Dua upon encountering an enemy or those of authority (1)

اللّهُـمَّ إِنا نَجْـعَلُكَ في نُحـورِهِـم، وَنَعـوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شُرورِهـمْ

Allāhumma innā naj`aluka fī nuḥūrihim wa na`ūdhu bika min shurūrihim.

O Allah, we ask You to restrain them by their necks, and we seek refuge in You from their evil.

Source: Abu Dawud 2/89, and Al-Hakim graded it authentic and Ath-Thahabi agreed 2/142.; Ḥiṣn al-Muslim 126

Dua upon encountering an enemy or those of authority (2)

اللّهُـمَّ أَنْتَ عَضُـدي، وَأَنْتَ نَصـيري، بِكَ أَجـولُ وَبِكَ أَصـولُ وَبِكَ أُقـاتِل

Allāhumma anta `aḍudī, wa anta naṣīrī, bika ajūlu, wa bika aṣūlu, wa bika uqātil.

O Allah, You are my strength and You are my support. For Your sake, I go forth and for Your sake, I advance and for Your sake, I fight.

Source: Abu Dawud 3/42, At-Tirmidhi 5/572. See also Al-Albani, Sahih At-Tirmidhi 3/183.; Ḥiṣn al-Muslim 127

Dua upon encountering an enemy or those of authority (3)

حَسْبُـنا اللهُ وَنِعْـمَ الوَكـيل

Ḥasbunallāhu wa ni`mal-wakīl.

Allah is sufficient for us and the best of those on whom to depend.

Source: Al-Bukhari, 5/172.; Ḥiṣn al-Muslim 128
