Dua for expelling the devil and his whisperings

Fortress of the Muslim (Ḥiṣn al-Muslim) · 3 duas in Arabic with transliteration, meaning and source

Dua for expelling the devil and his whisperings (1)

الاستعاذة بالله منه

A`ūdhu billāhi minash-shayṭānir-rajīm.

Seeking refuge with Allah against him (i.e. by saying I seek refuge in Allah from Satan the outcast).

Source: Abu Dawud 1/206, At-Tirmidhi. See also Al-Albani, Sahih At-Tirmidhi 1/77, and Surat Al-Mu'minun, 23:98-9.; Ḥiṣn al-Muslim 141

Dua for expelling the devil and his whisperings (2)

الْأَذَانُ

-- The call to prayer - 'Athan.

Source: Muslim 1/291, Al-Bukhari 1/151.; Ḥiṣn al-Muslim 142

Dua for expelling the devil and his whisperings (3)

الْأَذْكَارُ وَقِرَاءَةُ الْقُرْآنِ

-- Saying words of Allah's remembrance (Thikr) and recitation of the Qur'an.

Source: "Do not turn your homes into graveyards, surely the Devil flees from the house in which Surat Al-Baqarah is read," Muslim 1/539. The Devil is also driven out by the invocations for morning and evening, those that are said before sleeping and upon waking up, those for entering and leaving the house, including those for entering and leaving the mosque, and by many other authentic invocations taught to us by the Prophet (ﷺ) such as the reading of 'Ayatul-Kursi, (Al-Baqarah 2:255), and the last two 'Ayat of Surat Al-Baqarah before going to sleep. Whoever says: "There is none worthy of worship but Allah alone, Who has no partner, His is the dominion and His is the praise, and he is Able to do all things," one hundred times, it will be a protection for him from the Devil throughout the day.'; Ḥiṣn al-Muslim 143
